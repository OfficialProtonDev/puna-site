The rules changed in 2025. The deadlines are set.

The Water Services Act was amended in August 2025. Here's what that means for supplies that haven't registered yet.

  1. Sep 2025
    New Acceptable Solutions

    Self-supplied Buildings, Small to Medium Networks and Mixed-use Rural come into force.

  2. Sep 2026
    Old Acceptable Solutions stop counting

    Suppliers on the 2022 roof water, spring & bore or mixed-use rural solutions must have moved across.

  3. 1 Jul 2027
    New Quality Assurance Rules

    The 2026 Rules take effect and the compliance year becomes July to June.

  4. Nov 2028
    Register your supply

    Existing supplies that aren't registered must register with Taumata Arowai.

  5. Nov 2030
    Meet every obligation

    Previously unregistered supplies must be fully compliant. Safe water is required now.

Common questions

Is Puna part of Taumata Arowai?+

No. Puna is independent. You still register and report through Taumata Arowai's Hinekōrako portal – Puna prepares what you need for it.

We only supply a few people. Does this apply to us?+

Household supplies serving 25 or fewer people are no longer regulated. Marae, schools, halls, businesses and anything open to the public are, whatever their size. The free check tells you in a few minutes.

Do we need a drinking water safety plan?+

Not if you follow one of the 2025 Acceptable Solutions, or if you serve 25 or fewer people. Most supplies following the Quality Assurance Rules do – Puna drafts it from what you've entered.

What does complying cost?+

Mostly treatment equipment and lab tests. The free check shows a rough lab budget for your supply, so a committee can plan for it.

What happens to our records if we stop paying?+

They stay yours. You can always log in to view and export everything – you'll need it, because records must be kept for at least three years.
