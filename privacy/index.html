Privacy policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

Puna helps drinking water suppliers in Aotearoa New Zealand meet their obligations. This policy explains what personal information we collect and how we handle it, in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Organisation and supply details you enter: supply descriptions, test results, inspection and maintenance records, plans and documents.
  • Details of connected properties that network suppliers choose to record (names, addresses, emails, phone numbers) so they can send required consumer information.
  • If you use our free check and ask us to email the result: your email address, optional name and your answers.
  • Billing details are handled by our payment provider (Stripe). We don't store card numbers.
  • Basic technical information needed to run the service securely, such as IP addresses in server logs.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: build your compliance plan, send reminders, store your records and generate documents.
  • To send service emails (reminders, security and billing messages). You can turn reminder emails off.
  • To send occasional product updates if you asked for them – you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To improve Puna, using aggregated information that doesn't identify you.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't share your records with Taumata Arowai or anyone else unless you choose to, or the law requires it.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored with our hosting provider and protected with encryption in transit. Some of our service providers (hosting, email delivery and payments) may store information outside New Zealand; we only use providers with comparable privacy safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep your records while you have an account so you can meet record-keeping obligations (generally at least three years). You can export your records at any time, and ask us to delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you. Contact us at privacy@punawater.nz. If you're not happy with our response you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.
