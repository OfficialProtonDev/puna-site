What does compliance cost anyway?

For most small supplies the big costs are a filter + UV system (typically a few thousand dollars) and lab tests (often a few hundred dollars a year). Puna's free check shows a rough lab budget for your supply.

Can funding help?

Marae and rural communities have been able to access government drinking water funding programmes. Puna's records and plans make funding applications and reporting easier.
