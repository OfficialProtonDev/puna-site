The 2026 Quality Assurance Rules: what changes on 1 July 2027

Updated 24 Sept 2026

The Water Services (Drinking Water Quality Assurance) Rules 2026 were made in June 2026 and come into force on 1 July 2027. They replace the Drinking Water Quality Assurance Rules 2022 (revised 2024).

For small and medium supplies

  • The rule modules for very small communities (up to 25 people), small supplies (26–100) and medium supplies (101–500) are largely unchanged.
  • The compliance year changes from the calendar year to 1 July – 30 June.
  • Most detailed changes apply to large supplies (more than 500 people) and level 3 rules.

Transitional reporting in 2027

  • Quarterly reports for April–June 2027 are due by 28 July 2027.
  • Annual reporting for 1 January – 30 June 2027 (including rules T1.1, T1.2 and D1.1 for small supplies) is due by 22 September 2027.
  • From then on, annual reports cover 1 July – 30 June and are due within 40 working days.
